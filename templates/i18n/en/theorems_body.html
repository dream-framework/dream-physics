Operational readouts. Integer charges and instanton numbers; selection rules from class arithmetic; anomaly cancellation as a consistency constraint.

T3 — Coherence Threshold (Retention Law) Confirmed

A coherence scale \( \lambda_q \) separates regimes: below, interference-grade invariants retain; above, coarse-graining dominates.

\[ R(\lambda)=\exp\!\Big[-\big(\tfrac{\lambda}{\lambda_q}\big)^{D_{\mathrm{eff}}}\Big],\qquad \ln(-\ln R)=D_{\mathrm{eff}}\ln\lambda-D_{\mathrm{eff}}\ln\lambda_q. \]

Operational readouts. Two channels follow the same law with their own \( \lambda_q,D_{\mathrm{eff}} \): HF power \(R_{\mathrm{HF}}(\lambda)\) and structural correlation \(R_{\mathrm{struct}}(\lambda)=\mathrm{corr}^2(e_4|_{\lambda\to0},e_4|_\lambda)\).

Operational readouts. Elevated retention in high-\(A(x)\) regions; slower decay of \(R_{\mathrm{struct}}\) vs \(R_{\mathrm{HF}}\) consistent with web-like skeleton persistence.

T6 — Cross-domain Ranked-Spectrum Ratio Law Partially confirmed

The cumulative retained modes scale as \(N(\Omega)\!\sim\!\Omega^{D_{\mathrm{eff}}}\); ranked energies/masses obey the ratio law independent of base:

\[ N(\Omega)\sim\Omega^{D_{\mathrm{eff}}},\quad \rho(\Omega)\sim\Omega^{D_{\mathrm{eff}}-1},\qquad \frac{m_n}{m_1}=n^{1/D_{\mathrm{eff}}}. \]

Operational readouts. Fit \(D_{\mathrm{eff}}\) from retained-mode counts and check the ranked-ratio prediction where applicable (note: base \(m_1\) is context-set; the ratio is the invariant part).
