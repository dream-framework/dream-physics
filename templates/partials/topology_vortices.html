What you’re seeing

In Emergent mode you set the vacuum class (here U(1)) and the quench scale τQ. The field picks a phase in patches of size ξ* (set by τQ), and vortices (+1) / anti-vortices (−1) appear where patches mismatch—no hand placing. Increasing λ blurs the view; close ± pairs become unresolvable past the cliff (dashed tick), so the displayed Q(λ) drops. Switch to Manual to place/drag defects yourself.
